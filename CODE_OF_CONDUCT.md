# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Free Pool Radar to be a place where anyone can contribute — to add a
provider, correct a figure, improve the docs, or build on the data — without
being made to feel unwelcome for their experience level, background or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be **accurate**. This project's whole value is that its claims are true. Cite
  a source; date a figure. If you are unsure, say so.
- Be **kind**. Disagree about a technique, never about a person.
- Be **patient**. Reviewers are volunteers and this is a side project.
- Be **honest about uncertainty**. "I don't know" and "we couldn't verify that"
  are respected answers here, not failures.
- Respect the project's core rules in [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md) — in
  particular, that a failed source is never an ended offer.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Submitting fabricated data, or knowingly false evidence URLs.
- Adding ranking, "best", or quality-score language against the project's
  stated invariant.
- Repeatedly ignoring a maintainer's request to stop a behaviour.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space, as
well as when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/vikramdex-ops/free-pool-radar/security/advisories/new)
or emailing the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a contributor, at their
discretion, in proportion to the behaviour.
